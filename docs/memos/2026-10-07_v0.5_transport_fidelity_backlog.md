# v0.5 backlog: CATRINE benchmark follow-ups (2026-10-07)

## Guiding principles

1. **Physics documented, justified, readable.** Every physics and numerics
   step is documented: what is computed, the equations, why this choice,
   references, and how it differs from TM5 and GCHP. The code reads well for
   scientists. Documentation comes first for new or changed steps.
2. **GEOS-Chem is a reference, not the physical truth.** Closing the gap to
   GCHP is useful but not the goal in itself. Which vertical-transport choices
   are physically right is open in the community and will be decided within
   the team. Choices stay configurable and documented, and are judged against
   met-model diagnostics (e.g. the met model's own ω) and observations where
   possible.
3. **Algorithm differences before tuning.** First remove differences in the
   algorithms (closure, vertical and horizontal schemes). A collaborator will
   run GCHP in pure-advection mode; compare it with our advection-only run on
   the same met.

## Agreed order (2026-10-07)

1. Column closure with ΔB weights (item 1), as TM5 and GCHP effectively do,
   in all preprocessing paths.
2. FV3 `kord`-style vertical PPM (items 7–8).
3. A true FV3-equivalent advection path: `linrood` with a proper vertical
   step and a `hord 12`-style limiter (items 9–10).
4. Team decision: keep the Poisson pressure fixer, or add a GCHP-style mode
   without it (item 1c).
5. Advection-only comparison with the collaborator's GCHP run (item 12a).

Throughout: documentation and readability (section F).

## A. Preprocessing: vertical mass flux

0. **open, high priority — the dry-mass pin target inflates dry air by
   0.42 %.**
   - Global-mean dry surface pressure: GCHP `Met_PSC2DRY` 98,324.8 Pa
     (constant through 2022; `Met_AD` gives 98,323.8 Pa); our MERRA-2 run
     98,733.1 Pa. That is 1.00415 at every time checked, and the source of
     the "dry-air mass 1.0042 × `Met_AD`".
   - Cause: `[mass_fix] target_ps_dry_pa = 98726` (shared with the ERA5 path,
     5.135e18 kg) is Trenberth & Smith's dry-air mass converted to pressure
     with constant g and a spherical Earth. Their mean dry surface pressure is
     983.05 hPa; MERRA-2's is 983.25 hPa.
   - Effects: emitted mass dilutes into 0.4 % more air (enhancements about
     0.4 % weaker), transport is about 0.4 % slower for the same mass fluxes,
     and the burden ratios differ. It may contribute to tropospheric slopes
     of 0.96–0.99 against GCHP (not verified).
   - Fix: pin to the met data's own mean dry surface pressure (MERRA-2 about
     98,325 Pa; measure ERA5's), or remove only the drift.

1c. **open — pressure fixer vs GCHP.** GCHP corrects no horizontal fluxes.
   The column mismatch goes into the advected surface pressure, the pressure
   is reset from the met PS every 600 s, and tracer mass is rescaled
   globally (troposphere only;
   `fvdycore/model/fv_tracer2d.F90`, `offline_tracer_advection`). Its implied

## G. CATRINE protocol compliance (D7.1 §3.2)

Protocol: `~/data/AtmosTransport/catrine/protocol/CATRINE-D7.1-V1.pdf`. Every
3 hours, submissions need 3-D dry mole fractions (we provide these), surface
pressure, PBL height, tropopause height, and column tracer fluxes for budget
closure (dm_c/dt = −D + E − S). They must also explain how the PBL and
tropopause heights are computed.

28. **open — column tracer fluxes FE, FN and divergence D.** These are
    time-integrated over 3 h (kg/m and kg/m²): FE = (1/g)∫₀^ps u·q dp, FN
    likewise, D = ∇·(FN + FE). Accumulate them from the tracer fluxes the
    advection actually applies (face mass flux × reconstructed face value,
    summed over sweeps and substeps), so that D closes the column budget
    exactly. Rotate the cube-face components to east/north for FE and FN; D
    is a scalar. Optional: the same for water vapour, plus 3-D specific
    humidity.
29. **open — PBL height and tropopause height outputs**, with documented
    definitions. PBL height: the met field in the binary (MERRA-2 A1 PBLH;
    ERA5 BLH), or the height diagnosed by our PBL scheme. Tropopause: a WMO
    lapse-rate definition from T, or the met product's tropopause field.
    Pick one and document it (section F).
30. **open — compare column fluxes with GCHP.** GCHP's 3-hourly folder
    (https://data.nas.nasa.gov/catrine/CATRINE_C90/3hrly/, skipped at the
    start) holds its FN, FE and D. Comparing D isolates column-integrated
    horizontal transport; it complements the advection-only GCHP run
    (item 12a).
