AtmosTransport v0.5 against GCHP: CATRINE C90 benchmark

Key points

    Agreement with GCHP

    How to read: statistics of the monthly-mean fields on the common C90 grid, area weighted, then averaged over the months (bias: mean; RMSE: root of the mean square; r: mean spatial correlation). Bias = run − GCHP. Bands are fractions of each column's dry-air mass counted from the surface, so they do not depend on the models' level sets. The GCHP spatial standard deviation sets the scale against which an RMSE is small or large.

    Column agreement month by month

    RMSE of the monthly-mean column fields against GCHP. December 2021 is the spin-up month.
