Time series

Monthly means of each tracer by band of the air mass and latitude region, and the day-by-day agreement of the 3-hourly fields with GCHP.

Each point is the mean over one day of a statistic computed on the 3-hourly instantaneous fields on the C90 grid (area weighted, global). R² measures how well the spatial pattern matches, independent of a uniform offset; bias and RMSE are in tracer units. Values move with the weather: a day with sharp fronts or strong convection over source regions raises the RMSE.
