All sites against NOAA observations: CO2

How to read: the protocol CO2 is not real CO2 (protocol initial state and fluxes), so a bias against the observations is expected for every model; the centred RMSE (bias removed), the correlation r and the seasonal amplitude are the meaningful skill measures, and GCHP is scored exactly like the v0.5 runs. In situ sites: daily means of the observations (hours with obs_flag = 1) against each model's daily mean at the site on the same days; flask sites: each model's 3-hourly series interpolated linearly in time to every sample. Seasonal amplitude: peak to trough of the linearly detrended monthly means of the matched values (at least 12 months).

Sites and sampling

Each model is sampled in the cubed-sphere cell whose centre is nearest to the site (C90 cells are about 100 km across). Lowest layer: the model's lowest layer (surface sites). Altitude: GCHP's layer heights (Height_asl) and dry pressures (Met_PMIDDRY) at each time give the dry pressure at the site's height; GCHP is interpolated in height, each AtmosTransport run in log dry pressure on its own column (its dry pressure follows from its air mass per area). Mauna Loa (MLO) is sampled at its altitude, the Park Falls tall tower (LEF) at the intake height above the model surface. A mountain or coastal site sees air that a 100 km cell averages away (local upslope flow, sea breezes, nearby sources), so single-site differences carry a representativeness error for every model.
