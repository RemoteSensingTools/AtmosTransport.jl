Vertical structure

Zonal means on dry pressure from 900 to 10 hPa: where in latitude and height the v0.5 runs differ from GCHP and from each other. The black line is GCHP's tropopause (mean of four 12 UTC samples per month).

How to read: each model is interpolated to fixed dry-pressure levels in log pressure on its own grid, then averaged over 4° latitude bins (area weighted). Levels below 900 hPa are left out because which columns reach them depends on each model's surface pressure; the near-surface layer is covered by the near-surface page. The difference panels share one colour scale.
