Maps

Monthly-mean maps of each band of the air mass: each v0.5 run minus GCHP and GCHP itself, and the difference between the two v0.5 runs. Hover or tap a map for values.

How to read: the difference maps share one colour scale (±98th percentile of the run − GCHP maps), so equal colours mean equal differences; the GCHP map uses its own scale (2nd–98th percentile). The fields are monthly means of the 3-hourly snapshots on the C90 grid, averaged onto 2° cells for display. ERA5 − MERRA-2 is the spread between two v0.5 runs that differ only in their meteorology and its physics (vertical grid, convection, boundary-layer mixing): a lower bound on the transport uncertainty of this model.
